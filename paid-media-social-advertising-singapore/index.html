FAQs

How Quickly Do Paid Media Campaigns Start Delivering Results? +
Paid media can start generating traffic and enquiries soon after launch, but stronger results usually require testing, optimisation, and enough data to understand which audiences, messages, and channels are performing best.
How Much Should Businesses Budget for Paid Advertising? +
The right budget depends on your goals, target market, competition level, platform choice, and expected cost per lead or conversion. GS ConsultPro can help review your objectives and recommend a practical starting budget based on your campaign needs.
Do You Write Ad Copy and Support Creative Direction? +
Yes. GS ConsultPro can help with ad copy, campaign messaging, offer positioning, and creative direction. Design asset production can be included or coordinated depending on the agreed scope.
How Do You Improve Lead Quality from Paid Ads? +
Lead quality can be improved by refining audience targeting, strengthening messaging, improving landing pages, adding qualification steps, and tracking which campaigns generate meaningful enquiries rather than just form submissions.
Do You Manage Campaigns Across Multiple APAC Markets? +
Yes. GS ConsultPro can support paid media campaigns targeting Singapore and other APAC markets. Campaign structure, messaging, targeting, and budget allocation can be adapted based on each market's audience behaviour and business objectives.
What Happens if Campaign Performance Drops? +
Campaign performance can change due to audience fatigue, competition, budget shifts, creative performance, or platform changes. We review the data, identify likely causes, and recommend adjustments to targeting, messaging, creative, bidding, or campaign structure.
Can You Take Over Campaigns That Are Already Running? +
Yes. GS ConsultPro can review existing campaigns, identify what is working, assess where budget may be wasted, and recommend improvements before making changes to campaign structure, targeting, messaging, or tracking.
Do You Run Retargeting Campaigns? +
Yes. Retargeting campaigns can help re-engage people who have visited your website, interacted with your content, or shown interest in your business. These campaigns can support lead nurturing, enquiry generation, and conversion opportunities.
