FAQs

FAQs About Off-Page SEO & Link Building Services in Singapore

How Long Does It Take For New Backlinks To Affect Rankings? +
Most newly acquired links begin influencing rankings within four to twelve weeks depending on how quickly Google crawls the linking page and reassesses your domain. Consistent link building produces compounding improvements over time rather than a single sudden jump.
What Makes A Backlink High Quality Versus Low Quality? +
A high-quality backlink comes from a website with genuine editorial standards, real organic traffic, strong domain authority, and topical relevance to your business. Low-quality links come from sites created solely to sell links, have no real audience, or are completely unrelated to your industry.
Is Guest Posting Still An Effective Link Building Strategy In Singapore? +
Yes, when done correctly. Guest posts on genuine niche publications with real readerships produce valuable editorial links and referral traffic. The key distinction is between publishing genuinely useful expert content on reputable sites and paying for placements on link farms that exist purely to sell backlinks.
Can Bad Backlinks Hurt Our Rankings And What Can Be Done About Them? +
Yes, a backlink profile dominated by spammy or manipulative links can trigger ranking penalties. We conduct a full audit of your existing links as part of our process and can identify and disavow harmful links that may be suppressing your current performance before building new authority on top of a clean foundation.
How Many Links Do Singapore Businesses Need To See Ranking Improvements? +
There is no fixed number. The links required depend on the authority gap between your domain and the competitors currently outranking you. We benchmark your profile against top Singapore and APAC competitors first and recommend a monthly link volume calibrated to close that specific gap efficiently.
Do You Build Links To Inner Pages Or Only The Homepage? +
Both, depending on your ranking priorities. Homepage links strengthen overall domain authority while deep links to specific service or product pages concentrate ranking power where commercial value is highest. For most Singapore businesses we build a combination targeting both domain-level authority and page-level ranking improvements simultaneously.
Can You Build Links For Businesses Operating Across Multiple APAC Markets? +
Yes. We run regional link building campaigns covering Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and other Southeast Asian markets, sourcing locally relevant publications in each territory so your backlink profile carries genuine authority signals across every APAC market your business actively targets.
How Do You Report On Link Building Progress Each Month? +
Monthly reports detail every link acquired, the domain authority and traffic metrics of each linking site, the anchor text used, and the ranking movement observed across target keywords. Singapore and APAC clients receive full transparency on every placement, no aggregated summaries or withheld source information.
