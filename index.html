What Is the Difference Between SEO and Google Ads for Businesses? +

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) builds long-term organic visibility through content strategy, technical optimisation, and search authority, helping businesses generate sustainable traffic and reduce long-term acquisition costs.

Google Ads provides immediate paid search visibility, targeted traffic, and measurable lead generation through ongoing advertising campaigns.

For many businesses, the strongest digital growth strategy combines both SEO for sustainable long-term visibility and Google Ads for immediate traffic, conversions, and demand generation.

How Long Does SEO Take to Show Results? +
SEO typically delivers measurable improvements within 3 to 6 months, depending on factors such as competition, website authority, technical performance, and content consistency. More competitive industries may require longer-term investment to achieve strong organic visibility and sustainable ranking growth. GS ConsultPRO focuses on structured SEO strategies designed to strengthen search visibility, build topical authority, and support long-term business growth.
What Is Topical Authority and Why Does It Matter for SEO? +
Topical authority is the process of building comprehensive, trusted expertise around a specific subject so search engines recognise a website as a credible source within that area. This is achieved through strategically structured content that covers related topics, search intent, user questions, and key subject areas in depth. For businesses, strong topical authority improves organic visibility, strengthens rankings across competitive keywords, reduces dependence on paid traffic, and supports long-term SEO growth.
