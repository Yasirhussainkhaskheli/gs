Do I Need Technical SEO or Content SEO? +
It depends on what is limiting your website performance. Technical SEO focuses on how well your website can be crawled, indexed, loaded, and understood by search engines. Content SEO focuses on keywords, page structure, search intent, and content quality. Many websites need a combination of both.
How Long Does SEO Take to Show Results? +
SEO usually takes time to build. Some improvements may be visible within a few months, especially after technical fixes or page optimisation, but stronger traffic and enquiry results often take longer. The timeline depends on your website authority, competition level, content quality, and consistency of optimisation.
What Is the Difference Between SEO and Google Ads for Businesses? +

SEO (Search Engine Optimisation) builds long-term organic visibility through content strategy, technical optimisation, and search authority, helping businesses generate sustainable traffic and reduce long-term acquisition costs.

Google Ads provides immediate paid search visibility, targeted traffic, and measurable lead generation through ongoing advertising campaigns.

For many businesses, the strongest digital growth strategy combines both SEO for sustainable long-term visibility and Google Ads for immediate traffic, conversions, and demand generation.
