FAQs

How Is E-Commerce SEO Different From Standard Singapore Website Seo? +
E-commerce SEO addresses challenges standard Singapore websites never face including managing thousands of product URLs, handling duplicate content from filtering systems, and allocating crawl budget efficiently across large catalogues requiring specialist platform-specific knowledge to resolve correctly.
How Long Before Our Singapore Online Store Starts Ranking Organically On Google? +
Singapore product pages targeting lower-competition keywords typically begin ranking within six to ten weeks of optimisation. Competitive commercial category keywords usually take three to six months to show significant measurable movement in local Singapore search results.
Do You Work With All Singapore E-Commerce Platforms Or Only Specific Ones? +
We work across all major Singapore e-commerce platforms including Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Magento, and custom-built stores adapting our optimisation approach to each platform's specific technical architecture and ranking limitations.
Can E-Commerce SEO Reduce Our Singapore Paid Advertising Dependence Over Time? +
Yes. As Singapore organic rankings strengthen a growing proportion of monthly store revenue arrives without media spend attached progressively improving overall profitability and reducing vulnerability to paid platform cost increases affecting your Singapore store.
Should Singapore E-Commerce Stores Prioritise Product Pages Or Category Pages First? +
Category pages typically deliver faster and broader ranking impact because they target higher-volume commercial keywords covering multiple products simultaneously. We audit your specific store and prioritise based on revenue potential, most Singapore stores benefit from fixing category pages first then systematically optimising individual product pages underneath them.
How Do You Handle Duplicate Content Issues Common In Singapore E-Commerce Stores? +
Duplicate content from product variants, filtering systems, and pagination is one of the most damaging technical issues Singapore online stores face. We implement canonical tags, adjust crawl directives, and restructure URL parameters so Google indexes only the correct version of each page without splitting ranking signals unnecessarily.
Do You Optimise Product Descriptions Written By Our Singapore Suppliers Or Manufacturers? +
Yes. Manufacturer descriptions are typically duplicated across multiple Singapore and international retailers, making them useless for ranking purposes. We rewrite every priority product description around local buying-intent keywords with unique content that differentiates your Singapore store from competitors using identical supplier-provided copy across their catalogues.
Can E-Commerce SEO Work Alongside Our Existing Singapore Google Shopping Campaigns? +
Yes, and the two channels reinforce each other effectively. Organic product rankings and Google Shopping ads both appear for the same high-intent queries, increasing your Singapore store's total search visibility. Keyword performance data from Shopping campaigns also directly informs which product pages to prioritise in the organic optimisation programme.
