Common Blogging Mistakes That Hurt Your Search Rankings

Most Singapore businesses and APAC brands publishing blog content regularly are making structural mistakes that silently suppress their rankings. The problem is rarely the writing quality, it is the absence of search intent alignment, topic discipline, and a clear optimisation framework guiding every published piece.

Inconsistency

Irregular publishing gaps signal low site activity to search engines g,radually weakening the crawl frequency and ranking momentum your blog has built.

Shallowness

Surface-level articles that skim topics without genuine depth fail to satisfy search intent, resulting in poor dwell time and suppressed ranking positions.

Cannibalisation

Multiple posts targeting identical keywords compete against each other internally, dividing ranking authority instead of concentrating it behind one well-optimised, high-performing page.

FAQs About Blog Writing Services

Do You Handle Keyword Research Or Do We Need To Provide Topics? +
We handle the full research process, identifying target keywords, evaluating search volume and ranking difficulty, and building an editorial brief for every article before writing begins. You can also provide topics if you have specific areas you want to address, and we will validate and optimise them accordingly.
How Long Does It Take To See Organic Results From Blog Content? +
New articles typically begin appearing in search results within four to eight weeks of publication. Meaningful ranking improvements and traffic gains generally become visible between months three and six, depending on keyword competition and the domain authority of your site at the time of publishing.
What Industries Do You Write Blog Content For? +
We write for businesses across professional services, financial services, technology, e-commerce, healthcare, education, and property, with particular experience producing content for Singapore-based companies and APAC brands operating across multiple Southeast Asian markets.
How Long Are The Articles You Produce? +
Article length is determined by what top-ranking competitors are publishing for each specific keyword, not by a fixed word count target. Most articles we produce fall between 1,000 and 2,500 words, with long-form educational pieces extending beyond that where search intent and topic complexity require it.
Can You Write In Our Brand Voice? +
Yes. We begin every new client engagement with a brand voice briefing, reviewing your existing content, tone guidelines, and audience expectations so that every article we produce reads as a natural extension of your existing communication style.
Do You Publish The Articles Directly To Our Website? +
We can deliver finished articles as documents for your team to upload, or we can manage the full publishing process directly within your CMS, including formatting, image placement, internal linking, and meta data entry, based on your preference and access level.
What Is The Difference Between Blog Writing And Content Marketing Strategy? +
Blog writing is the execution, producing individual articles to a defined brief. Content marketing strategy is the planning layer that determines which topics to cover, in what order, at what depth, and for which funnel stages. Ideally both work together, and we offer both as standalone or combined services.
How Many Blog Posts Do We Need To Publish Each Month? +
Consistency matters more than volume. For most Singapore businesses, four to six well-researched, properly optimised articles per month produces stronger cumulative results than publishing daily thin content with no strategic intent or keyword alignment behind it.
