FAQs

How much does it cost to build an e-commerce website in Singapore? +
E-commerce website development costs in Singapore vary based on platform, number of products, and required integrations. Most projects range from a straightforward Shopify setup to a fully custom-built store. We provide a detailed, transparent proposal after understanding your specific requirements.
How long does it take to launch an online store? +
A standard e-commerce store typically takes 4 to 8 weeks from project kickoff to launch. Larger stores with custom functionality, marketplace integrations, or complex product catalogues may require 8 to 14 weeks for full delivery.
Which e-commerce platform is best for my Singapore business? +
The right platform depends on your product range, budget, and growth plans. Shopify suits most small to mid-size retailers, WooCommerce offers greater flexibility for content-heavy stores, and BigCommerce is ideal for enterprise-level operations requiring advanced scalability.
Will my online store be mobile-friendly and SEO-optimised? +
Yes. Every e-commerce store we build is mobile-first by design and includes full technical SEO foundations, clean URL structures, optimised meta data, fast load speeds, and structured product schema, ensuring strong visibility on Google from launch.
Can you migrate my existing online store to a new platform? +
Absolutely. We manage full platform migrations including product data, customer records, order history, and URL redirects, ensuring zero data loss and protecting your existing search rankings throughout the entire migration process.
Do you offer support and maintenance after the store goes live? +
Yes. We offer ongoing support and maintenance packages covering security updates, platform upgrades, performance monitoring, and content edits to keep your online store running at its best long after launch day.
